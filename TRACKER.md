## 🚨 Red Flags to Watch

### Development Red Flags
- ⚠️ Task taking >2 days longer than estimated
- ⚠️ Code coverage dropping below 70%
- ⚠️ More than 5 critical bugs
- ⚠️ Team member blocked for >1 day
- ⚠️ Merge conflicts happening frequently

### Business Red Flags
- ⚠️ Budget overrun >10%
- ⚠️ Timeline slipping >1 week
- ⚠️ Stakeholder not responding to demos
- ⚠️ Client feedback negative
- ⚠️ Team morale low

**Action**: If you see 2+ red flags, escalate to Project Manager immediately!

## 💡 Pro Tips

### For Developers
- ✅ Commit code at least 2x per day
- ✅ Write tests BEFORE code (TDD)
- ✅ Ask for help if blocked >2 hours
- ✅ Review code within 24 hours
- ✅ Document as you go

### For Project Manager
- ✅ Update this tracker daily
- ✅ Check in with team members 1-on-1 weekly
- ✅ Celebrate small wins
- ✅ Address blockers immediately
- ✅ Keep stakeholders informed

### For Everyone
- ✅ Communicate early and often
- ✅ Be honest about progress
- ✅ Help teammates when possible
- ✅ Focus on quality over speed
